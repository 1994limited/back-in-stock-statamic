# Back in Stock

**"Tell me when it's back" for Cargo.** Shoppers leave their email on a sold-out product, or a sold-out variant, and get one email when it's back in stock. You see what people are waiting for, and who bought when it came back.

## What it does

- **A form for sold-out products and variants.** One tag, your markup. It shows only while it's sold out, works without JavaScript and on statically cached pages, and follows the size, colour or edition chosen on the page.
- **Confirmed by email.** Every request is confirmed with a button, so nobody can sign someone else up, and link checkers can't confirm for them. A consent tick box keeps the wording people agreed to.
- **Restocks noticed however they happen.** Stock changed in the Control Panel, put back by a cancelled order, or changed by an import: Back in Stock notices and emails people, oldest request first.
- **Never twice, never too many.** Each request is emailed once. Email everyone, or as many people as units came back. A delay lets you fix a mistyped stock level, and a per-minute limit keeps your mail provider happy.
- **One-click unsubscribe**, as Gmail and Yahoo ask for.
- **Demand in the Control Panel.** The most wanted sold-out products, who's waiting, each restock, and how many bought after the email. A dashboard widget and CSV download.
- **Privacy built in.** Find, download or delete everything about an address. Old requests are deleted on a schedule.
- **Multisite.** Emails go in the language of the site people asked on.
- **Spam protection.** A honeypot, rate limits per visitor and per address.
- **Yours to change.** Wording, logo and colours in the Control Panel; publish the email views to change anything else. Events for syncing to your mailing list.

Antlers and Blade examples for everything, and a demo shop to copy from.

## Requirements

Statamic 6.35.1+, Cargo 1.15+, PHP 8.3+, a database, and Laravel's scheduler.
